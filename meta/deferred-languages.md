## Rationale for the Round 2 cap

The seven languages chosen for Round 2 (Mandarin, Japanese, Russian, German, Korean, Spanish, French) were selected by:

1. **Volume of aging-relevant literature absent from English databases** (CNKI, J-STAGE, eLibrary.ru are large indexes with significant non-overlap with PubMed).
2. **Presence of formal abstract databases with structured search** (LILACS for Spanish, HAL for French, etc.).
3. **AI translation reliability at the Q1–Q2 level** for biomedical text in 2026.

Adding more languages now would dilute attention without adding much additional evidence; the marginal language in Round 2 (French) is already in a regime of diminishing returns.

If reviewers or readers raise specific claims pointing to evidence in deferred languages, those claims will be tracked in `meta/queue.md` for Round 3.
